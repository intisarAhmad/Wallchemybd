What we make

Three materials, worked until they behave like one.

Every panel starts as a flat board and ends as a piece of wall furniture — textured by hand, wired for warm light, drilled for water.

SANDSTONE

Texture you can read from across the room

A fine mineral grain is troweled over the panel face and cured, so the surface catches light the way plaster does — matte, uneven, alive. Borderless edges, no frame.

LIGHT

A halo, not a lamp

Warm 3000K LEDs sit behind the panel and wash the wall instead of your eyes. Plug-in with an inline switch; remote-dimmable on selected pieces.

WATER

A vase that stays planted

A borosilicate tube drops through the shelf and holds real water. Root a pothos cutting, drop in a single stem, or leave it empty — it reads as sculpture either way.

Collection

The detail

Built to hang once and stay put.

Panels are light enough for a single nail on brick or a plug on plaster. The vase lifts out for cleaning, and the LED runs cool enough to leave on all evening.

How to order

Three steps, and it happens in Messenger.

No account, no checkout form. Pick a piece and we finish the details in a chat, the way you would with any shop on Facebook.

Choose your panel

Open any piece, pick the size and finish you want, and check the dimensions against your wall.

Send it to our page

Tap Order on Messenger. Your choices get copied to the clipboard and our chat opens — paste and send.

Confirm and receive

We reply with availability and delivery. Cash on delivery in Dhaka, bKash or bank transfer elsewhere.
